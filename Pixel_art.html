"LLYOD IS GOOD"

One of the most iconic quotes in this manhwa. You can find it in the hell arc around chapter 95–110. Llyod (the MC) helps a village experiencing drought, but since the villagers don't trust him, he takes an unusual approach — convincing them that "water is good for life, Llyod gives water, so Llyod is good." Read it yourself to understand what "unusual" really means.

The Extra's Academy Survival Guide ↗

The Extra's Academy Survival Guide

The characters in this pixel art are Ed Rosthaylor (the yellow-haired guy), the MC by the way, and Lucy Mayreel (the magical girl). In the lore, Lucy was close to Ed at first because Edd had a cabin in the academy forest and Lucy really liked sleeping or relaxing around the cabin.
Context of the dialogue in Lucy's bubble chat: at the beginning of the story, the reader does not know the name or surname of Ed (MC), where in the dialogue, Ed's last name is censored like this "Ed *******or" so that the readers, or perhaps more precisely, readers in Indonesia, name Ed's last name according to their wishes as a joke, they say "the important thing is that the end of his name is 'or'."

BURGER BANGOR ↗

BURGER BANGOR GRILL BOX DESIGN

I created this packaging design for a competition held by Bangor Burger, a fairly large local burger chain in Indonesia.
I created this design using only four colors,

  • GreenYellow(97CC00FF)
  • Sage Green(8BB68AFF)
  • Olive Green(54612BFF)
  • Black Out(222222FF)

as per their requirements, taken from their main brand colors, with their chosen theme of "Bangor Culture Today (Share What We Live)." This theme focuses on people's activities with a Bangor burger as a companion. So, I created pixel art that illustrates people's activities like ordering food, going to or from work, and looking at computers—things we often encounter around us.

The Balcon (OG Art) ↗

The Balcon

This is an original piece of pixel art I created. It depicts someone gazing out at the bustling city while enjoying a cup of coffee on their balcony. I used a limited number of frames, giving the animation a somewhat rough look, but I really enjoy the piece; I even use it as my phone wallpaper since I designed the aspect ratio to fit mobile devices perfectly.
